# Rabbit holes — Yang–Mills with the spin-1 ghost, as of 2026-10-21

Not a handoff, not a context file, not a work order. A list of the holes we have looked into and backed out
of, so that nobody falls into one by accident and anyone who wants to go hunting knows where the entrances are.
Each entry: what was seen (with scope), where it was found, why it might matter, what it would cost, and its
status. Nothing here is needed for the paper as scoped (CONTEXT, item 6); the paper cites what was measured.
Numbers are from the named logs or scratch scripts; a scratch script is a session file, not a committed result.

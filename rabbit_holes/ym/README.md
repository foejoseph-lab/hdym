## The picture that came out

- **Linear stage.** (Frozen spectra and per-segment gains below are frame conventions on a periodic block, 24 Oct: only the multipliers and N are invariant.) The half-period reflection symmetry of the DR orbit makes M = N². A single ghost pair can
  therefore only fold at M = +1 (parametric); every −1 event is a Krein collision of two pairs of opposite
  signature at N ≈ ±i (lemma, hole 7; signatures measured in `islands/results/krein_b005.txt`). The two pairs
  are the same sector ghost twice, degenerate at low E up to the splitting Δ(E) (holes 5, 6, 2+7). Their band
  centres lie on a ladder in the orbit period (hole 2); the ladder fixes slope and count, not a frequency. The
  exact dynamics is the 4-dim gauge-reduced block with a light mode soft at the turning points and a heavy
  mode near 1–1.7 (2+7 cont.); in the strong Krein band the growth happens at the bounces in RH3's frame (hole 3; frame-bound, `notes/RH_reduced_block_transfer.md`).
- **Nonlinear stage.** The off-diagonal mode triggers, the diagonal ghost executes: it crosses amplitude 1
  first in 71–89 % of cells, the loiter after that is memoryless with a rate set by whichever sector pumped it
  (hole 4), the runaway does not remember which mechanism started it beyond a residual plane (hole 12), and
  the seed phase plays no role (hole 1). At the paper's seed size the linear bands no longer predict the escape
  at all (hole 8). The regular island is emptied by the off-diagonal sector at ε = 10⁻⁸, throughout its core, in ≈ 38 orbits (1340/35.28, median t_esc over T at E = 0.3; stated as 25 until 28 Oct 2026)
  (hole 9), and by the diagonal ghost's back-reaction above ε ≈ 5 × 10⁻⁸ (hole 10).
- **Family.** The physical coupling c_j = 1 is the least unstable member of the c_j family except the ghost-free
  c_j = 0: at c_j = 0.25 the block carries a double +1 fold over E ∈ [0.41, 1] with \|μ\| up to 34, a smooth
  single-humped exponent, going with a light reduced mode that is hyperbolic for up to half the period — though
  frozen hyperbolicity alone does not decide stability (2+7, `results/rh27_cj_scan.txt`,
  `results/rh_reduced_block_cj025.txt`, `results/rh_reduced_block_hyp.txt`).
